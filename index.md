I'm a postdoctoral researcher in neuroinformatics at the University of Cambridge on the <a href="https://www.globalfnirs.org/the-bright-project/" title="BRIGHT Project" target="_blank">BRIGHT Project</a>. I obtained <a href="./assets/resources/benerradi2023phd.pdf" title="PhD Thesis" target="_blank">my PhD</a> in Computer Science from the University of Nottingham (<a href="https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/research/groups/mixedrealitylab/" title="MRL" target="_blank">Mixed Reality Lab</a> & <a href="https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/research/groups/cvl/" title="CVL" target="_blank">Computer Vision Lab</a>) and my research focuses on machine learning and statistical modelling for <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Functional_near-infrared_spectroscopy" target="_blank">fNIRS</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electroencephalography" target="_blank">EEG</a>. I am the author of the <a href="https://hanbnrd.gitlab.io/benchnirs" title="BenchNIRS" target="_blank">BenchNIRS</a> and <a href="https://hanbnrd.github.io/NIRSimple/" title="NIRSimple" target="_blank">NIRSimple</a> Python packages.
